# Leesmaat

**A little reading. A bigger world.**

Leesmaat means a reading companion: a warm, quiet place to build confidence in English and Afrikaans. The name stays short, human and connected to the app's South African context.

The mark combines an open book with a small spark of understanding. The lower-case Literata wordmark carries a coral full stop. Use the book alone at small sizes and the wordmark when there is room. The maskable icon keeps the mark inside the central safe area.

- Forest green `#2f6b4f`: the core brand and actions.
- Paper `#fbfaf7`: page, browser and default launch background.
- Ink `#161614`: primary reading and navigation.
- Gold `#eab972`: curiosity, the spark and illustration accents.
- Coral `#e8643c`: the wordmark stop and streak accent.
- Literata for reading and editorial headings; Inter for controls.

Sepia uses a warm parchment background; night uses true black with softer green accents. Monochrome keeps the same hierarchy. Safe areas and surrounding browser space take the reading background, while screens and loading states use the same tokens.

Motion supports reading: subtle press feedback, a spring-like navigation indicator and gently turning covers. Three.js introduces the open book and an ambient world during placement. Reduced motion and data saver keep a quiet static composition. Avoid movement behind the actual reading text.

Covers are original editorial illustrations with six families: window, mountain, sea, forest, moon and city. Their title and author are readable; modern setworks say Study notes. Never suggest these are publisher artwork or complete copyrighted texts.

Obvious actions use an icon with an accessible label and a tooltip. Keep words for choices, unfamiliar actions and primary onboarding steps. All three main navigation destinations remain icon-only.

Assets: `public/icon.svg`, PNG and maskable icons, `public/brand/wordmark.svg`, `public/social.png`, `public/brand/splash/`, and `public/art/covers/`. Rebuild with `pnpm art`.
